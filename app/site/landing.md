# DelRio

FM-1_DelRio turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into a small rhythm box of hand percussion, made to accompany songs in a circle:
djembe, cajon, udu, clapsticks, maracas and triangle. The berimbau has its own page, with its toques and the capoeira's
bateria; two drones, the tanpura and the shruti box, sound alone or under the rhythm. By Jess Del Rio.

![DJOLE playing on the FM-1's screen: the djembe's strokes B, T and S on four rows of four steps, the first framed in white; 120 BPM, 4/4, DJEMBE and a green triangle in the footer; the kit's six instruments in three letters below](manual/img/screen-playing.png "DJOLE on the FM-1's screen")
